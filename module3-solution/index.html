Sweet Madame

Named for a beloved recipe from Mondstadt, Sweet Madame is a 2-star healing dish in Genshin Impact, known for being the first recipe players obtain and for its extremely simple ingredients. Sweet Madame is created by cooking two fowls and two sweet flowers. The effect restores 22% of Max HP and an additional 1,200 HP to the selected character.

Dinner of Judgment

Named for Rosaria's specialty dish, Dinner of Judgment is a 2-star healing dish in Genshin Impact, obtained by cooking Sweet Madame with Rosaria as the active character. It is created by cooking two fowls and two sweet flowers. The effect restores 30% of Max HP and an additional 1,750 HP to the selected character.

Steak

Steak is a 1-star revival dish in Genshin Impact, known for being the first revival food players learn and for requiring only a single ingredient — two raw meats. It is a simple grilled steak that revives a fallen character and restores a small amount of HP.

Outrider's Champion Steak

Outrider's Champion Steak! is a 1-star revival dish in Genshin Impact, and it is Amber's specialty. It is made by cooking Steak while Amber is the active character. It revives a fallen character and restores 10% of Max HP, then restores 1,200 HP.

Sushi

Tuna Sushi

Tuna Sushi is a 2-star healing dish in Genshin Impact, a traditional Inazuma recipe that can be purchased from Kiminami Anna in Inazuma City. Tuna Sushi is created by cooking three fish and three rice. The effect restores 22% of Max HP and an additional 1,200 HP to the selected character.

Mega-Meaty Sushi

Mega-Meaty Sushi is a 3-star healing dish in Genshin Impact, a collaboration recipe obtained from the Sushiro × Genshin Impact event. Mega-Meaty Sushi is created by cooking four raw meats, two radishes, and three flours. The effect restores 32% of Max HP and an additional 1,250 HP to the selected character.
