Our Menu

A journey through the eight culinary regions of Teyvat — from the hearty hearths of Mondstadt to the frost-kissed kitchens of Snezhnaya.

🍽️ Mondstadt

The City of Freedom eats the way it lives — simply, generously, and always outdoors. Expect hearty grilled meats, golden potatoes, and pastries baked fresh for the Knights of Favonius. Every plate here tastes of wind, wheat, and good company.

  • Grilled Meat Skewers / Pile 'Em Up: Juicy chunks of grilled meat served on skewers or stacked together with potatoes and vegetables for a hearty meal.
  • Fish-Flavored Toast: Thick, golden toast topped with fresh fish and vegetables, making a simple but filling meal.
  • Mondstadt Grilled Fish: Fresh fish grilled over an open flame and seasoned with herbs and spices. Diona's specialty is called Definitely Not an Appetizer.

🥢 Liyue

In the Harbor of Commerce, food is craftsmanship. Liyue's kitchens prize slow broth, precise technique, and dishes rich with meaning — each one a small blessing of prosperity for the table.

  • Crystal Shrimp: Delicate steamed dumplings filled with shrimp and wrapped in translucent rice dough.
  • Squirrel Fish: A crispy, deep-fried fish shaped to resemble a squirrel, covered in a sweet and sour sauce.
  • Universal Peace: A colorful rice dish decorated with fruits and vegetables, symbolizing prosperity, happiness, and good fortune.
  • Bamboo Shoot Soup: A comforting soup made with fresh bamboo shoots and meat, offering a light yet flavorful taste.

🍣 Inazuma

An island nation fed by the sea. Inazuma's cuisine is light, clean, and quietly refined — fresh catches, delicate noodles, and sweets that turn a simple snack into a small ceremony.

  • Sushi (Tuna, Egg, etc.): Bite-sized portions of seasoned rice topped or filled with ingredients such as fresh fish and egg.
  • Imported Poultry: A beautifully prepared poultry dish featuring tender meat and rich seasoning, known for its elegant presentation.
  • Soba Noodles: Traditional Japanese-style buckwheat noodles served in a warm, savory broth.
  • Tricolor Dango: Three soft, chewy rice dumplings in pink, white, and green, served on a wooden skewer.

🍛 Sumeru

Where the rainforest meets the desert, spice meets tradition. Sumeru's table draws from South Asian and Middle Eastern kitchens — fragrant rice, warm flatbreads, and stews that simmer for hours.

  • Shawarma Wrap: Thin slices of seasoned roasted meat wrapped in flatbread with vegetables and flavorful sauces.
  • Panipuri: Crispy, hollow fried dough balls filled with spicy, tangy water and flavorful fillings.
  • Biryani: Fragrant rice cooked with aromatic spices, vegetables, and tender meat for a rich and satisfying meal.
  • Padisarah Pudding: A delicate, creamy dessert with a smooth texture and floral-inspired presentation. It is Nilou's specialty dish.

🥐 Fontaine

Refinement is the rule in Fontaine. Sauces are built with care, desserts are almost too beautiful to eat, and even a simple plate of fish carries a touch of quiet elegance worthy of the Court of Justice.

  • Black Pudding: A savory sausage made using blood and other ingredients, traditionally cooked until firm and rich in flavor.
  • Fish and Chips: Golden, crispy battered fish served with thick-cut fried potatoes, a classic comfort food.
  • Macarons: Colorful French-style almond meringue cookies with delicate crisp shells and sweet, creamy fillings.
  • Steak Tartare: Finely chopped raw beef seasoned with herbs and spices, usually served with a carefully arranged garnish.

🔥 Natlan

Bold, rustic, and cooked over open flame. Natlan's food is built for the field — grilled meats, corn-based staples, and stews that warm you from the inside out after a long day under the sun.

  • Tataco: A tortilla-style wrap filled with savory ingredients, offering a flavorful handheld meal.
  • Grainfruit Meat Soup: A hearty, warming soup combining tender meat with grainfruit and other ingredients in a flavorful broth.
  • Flame Stew: A rich, spicy meat stew slowly cooked with vegetables and seasonings, perfect for a filling meal.

🌙 Nod-Krai

The youngest region on the map, and the coldest in spirit. Nod-Krai's kitchens lean Nordic — berries beside rich meat, simple street food, and desserts that taste like a quiet evening under a long moonlit sky.

  • Nod-Krai Hot Dog: A convenient street-food snack featuring a sausage served inside soft bread with flavorful toppings.
  • Berry Pan-Fried Meat: Tender meat cooked in a pan and paired with berries, creating a delicious balance of savory and sweet flavors.
  • Summer Hazel Cake: A sweet, delicate cake featuring hazelnut flavors, making it a lovely dessert or afternoon treat.

🍲 Snezhnaya

Cold-region cuisine built to survive the winter — heavy, warming, and deeply comforting. Snezhnayan food is meant to be shared around a fire, wherever one can be lit.

  • Snezhnaya Shashliks: Large chunks of marinated meat threaded onto iron skewers and grilled over an open fire until smoky and tender.
  • Borscht: A traditional beetroot soup with a vibrant red color, slow-cooked with vegetables and meat and often topped with sour cream.
  • Stroganina: Thin, delicate slices of frozen raw fish served cold, usually with a little salt to highlight their natural flavor.
  • Podvorye Smoked Sausage & Medovik: A combination of smoky, savory sausage and Medovik, a traditional Russian honey cake made with thin layers of honey-flavored pastry and creamy filling.
