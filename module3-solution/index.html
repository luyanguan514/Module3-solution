🍲 Snezhnaya

Cold-region cuisine built to keep the bitter cold at bay.

  • Snezhnaya Shashliks: Large chunks of marinated meat grilled on iron skewers. A common sight wherever a fire can be lit, from courtyard gatherings to hunting trips.
  • Borscht: A vibrant red beet soup slow-simmered with vegetables and meat, often served with a spoonful of sour cream.
  • Stroganina: Thin, curling strips of frozen raw fish, typically served simply with coarse salt.
  • Podvorye Smoked Sausage & Medovik: A smoked sausage dish and a classic honey cake, both available from the Podvorye Restaurant in Snezhnograd.
